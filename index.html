06 / The details

A few things
worth knowing.

Need a setup-specific answer?
Contact the team ↗

How do I order?

Orders are handled directly on Discord. Open a ticket to confirm your plan and receive payment instructions.

How do delivery and activation work?

The team provides delivery and activation instructions through your Discord ticket. Confirm the expected delivery time and activation conditions before payment.

Where can I get help?

Join the AERV Discord and open a support ticket. Include your Windows version and a description of the issue. Never post your license key in a public channel.

Does access renew automatically?

The displayed 7-day and 30-day plans are fixed-duration access, without automatic renewal.

Is the interface preview the actual application?

The interactive panel is an HTML demonstration based on the supplied interface. Controls affect this preview only. The films contain staged animation. Confirm current product capabilities before purchasing.

Is there a guarantee against game bans?

No. Third-party game tools can lead to detection and account penalties. AERV is independent and is not affiliated with Riot Games.

What about refunds and lifetime access?

Confirm the exact refund, transfer and lifetime-license terms with the team before any real purchase. Orders are arranged directly with the team on Discord.
